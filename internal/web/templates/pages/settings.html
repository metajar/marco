How detection works

Every check Marco pings the device, knocks on a few TCP ports, and asks the network who owns the address (ARP). Any answer means the phone is on the home Wi-Fi.

When a kid turns Wi-Fi off to use cellular data, all three go silent. After the configured number of missed checks the device is marked Off Wi-Fi, and if that happens during one of their schedules, you get a text.

A phone that's powered off or has a dead battery looks the same as Wi-Fi being turned off.
